Partner advertising with micianaron
micianaron gives businesses the opportunity to place their products and services in front of people while they are actively completing digital tasks. Our advertising philosophy is deliberately simple. Advertisements should support the platform without getting in the way of the reason someone visited it. We do not build our advertising experience around intrusive popups, forced interruptions, or advertisements that prevent someone from using a tool. Partner placements are kept separate from the functionality of the tools. A user should always be able to complete their task without being forced to interact with an advertisement. Advertising opportunities may include sponsored placements, brand campaigns, product promotion, business partnerships, and other commercial arrangements that fit the micianaron platform. Advertising availability, placement, pricing, campaign requirements, and other commercial terms may vary depending on the campaign and the requirements of micianaron. If your company is interested in advertising with micianaron, you can create a partner account and provide your business information through our registration process.
